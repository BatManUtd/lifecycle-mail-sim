Built-in Suppression Rules

These always apply, for every campaign, and cannot be edited or turned off (except the global cap, which is opt-in).

  • Unsubscribed — a contact who has unsubscribed never receives any campaign.
  • Already paid — payment_reminder campaigns are suppressed when payment_status is "paid".
  • Frequency cap exceeded — each campaign's own max-per-rolling-N-days cap, counted from that contact's send history (seeded from last_email_sent_date plus anything sent earlier in this simulation).
  • Global daily send cap — optional; caps total emails per contact per day across all campaigns.
